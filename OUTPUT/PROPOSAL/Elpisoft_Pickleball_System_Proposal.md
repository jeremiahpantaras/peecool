### The business need

A busy pickleball facility has many courts, many matches and a front desk that needs to know what is happening on every court. Paper score sheets, whiteboards and separate apps make it hard to see which courts are free, keep accurate results, and understand how the facility is being used.

## 2. Project overview

The Pickleball Court Management and Scoring System is a locally deployed system that simplifies court operations, match scoring, player management, match monitoring and facility reporting.

The Master System is the central control centre for the whole facility. Each court operates independently while staying in constant contact with the Master System through the facility’s local network. The system supports up to 24 courts, and the administrator decides how many are in use.

The system is designed for local operation. It does not need an active internet connection for its core scoring and management functions.

### Main system components

- **A. Master System.** The central management application on a designated Windows 11 computer. It hosts the system and its database, and it is where the Admin and Staff manage courts, matches, players, the booking schedule and reports.
- **B. Court Scoring System.** An independent scoring screen for each court, opened in the browser of a tablet, smartphone or other compatible device assigned to that court. It sends every point to the Master System in real time.

### System objectives

1. Give the front desk a live view of every court, match and score.
2. Let each court score its own matches quickly and accurately.
3. Keep a complete, reliable record of matches, players and results.
4. Help the owner understand court usage through clear reports.
5. Keep the facility running without depending on an internet connection.

## 5. Offline operation and system architecture

The system does not need the internet for normal facility operations.

> The system keeps working even when the facility has no internet connection. The Master Computer and the court devices talk to each other directly through the facility’s own local network, the same way devices in an office can share a printer without going online.

### How it works

1. The Master System is installed on a Windows 11 computer at the facility.
2. This Master PC runs the system and keeps the database on its own storage.
3. The Master PC connects to the facility’s network router with an Ethernet cable.
4. Court devices connect to the facility’s staff-only Wi-Fi network.
5. Each court device opens its own scoring screen in a web browser.
6. Scores and match information reach the Master System in real time.

### Internet and local network are not the same thing

- **Internet.** The connection from your facility to the outside world, provided by an internet service provider. Not needed for daily scoring and management.
- **Local network.** The private network inside your facility, made of your router and Wi-Fi access points. The system needs this to connect court devices to the Master PC.

**What the facility still needs.** The facility still needs a working router and Wi-Fi access points so the court devices can reach the Master PC. If the Master PC is switched off, or a court device loses the Wi-Fi, scoring on that court pauses until the connection returns. Nothing already saved is lost.

### Technical deployment details

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Operating system | Windows 11 |
| Database | SQLite, stored locally on the Master PC |
| Master PC network | Wired Ethernet connection |
| Court connectivity | Dedicated staff-only Wi-Fi |
| Master PC address | Fixed local IP address |
| Power protection | UPS recommended |
| Internet | Not required for core system operation |

Architecture: Master PC (Windows 11, SQLite, fixed local IP, UPS) → Ethernet → router and Wi-Fi access points → staff-only Wi-Fi → court devices (Court 1 … Court 24). Internet is optional and not needed for scoring.
