The Bounce

The symbol is a ball’s bounce path: a tapered motion trail coming down, an impact on the court line, and a perforated ball rising off the right arm. It also reads as a checkmark, a point confirmed. The wordmark stands on the same court line.

03 / TYPE

One family, three roles

Geist is a clean, neutral sans built for interfaces, with a matching mono. One family keeps the product coherent: Regular for headlines and reading, Bold with tabular figures for every number, Mono for technical text. All open-licence (SIL OFL) and self-hosted, since the system never touches the internet.

Chosen by test, not taste: tabular figures keep 11 and 88 the same width (zero drift on a ticking timer); digits are 0.75 em tall, so 48% of the container height gives 36% digit height (spec: ≥ 30%); and its digits are more distinct when blurred to simulate distance (worst pair 0.79, against 0.87 for the condensed face considered earlier). Console rules
  • Score digits: Geist Sans Bold, tabular, 48cqh ≈ 36% of the screen height. Contrast ≥ 7:1 in both themes.

Lines, dots and the bounce
