PEECOOL BRAND GUIDELINES · v1.002
01Brand strategy

Positioning

Positioning statement

For pickleball facilities that need to run many courts without losing a point, Peecool is the all-in-one management and scoring platform. Unlike booking-only tools or standalone scorekeepers, it runs on the facility’s own network, so scoring never depends on the internet.

Purpose

Make every game at a facility count: accurately scored, fairly organised, easy to run.

Mission

Give facilities one dependable system to run their courts, score every match and keep players moving.

Vision

The system facilities trust to run the game: pickleball first, ready for every court sport.

Personality, audience, values

Personality

ComposedCompetitivePreciseQuickOrganisedWelcoming

A professional sports-technology product, not wellness, retail or hospitality. The name’s “cool” is composure under pressure: the calm of a well-run court.

Audience (by role in the product)

  • Facility owners and managers (Admin): run everything, see analytics
  • Front-desk and court staff (Staff): operate courts, bookings, Open Play
  • Players: see it on court devices and the TV board; not users
  • The integrator (Elpisoft): deploys under the facility’s own name

Core values

  • Accuracy: every point can be corrected, nothing is lost
  • Speed: one tap, live on every screen
  • Fair play: transparent rules and rotations
  • Always on: your network, no internet needed
  • Everyone’s game: readable, accessible, easy to learn

01Brand story & voice

Keep your cool. Keep count.

Story

Pickleball facilities fill up fast: a dozen courts, a front desk, a queue of players, and a score someone has to get right. Peecool began as the system behind that moment. One Master PC, every court in sync, scores that survive a dropped Wi-Fi signal, and Open Play that sorts itself. The “P” is for pickleball. The “cool” is what it gives a facility on its busiest night.

Voice

  • Short, direct sentences. Real numbers, no hype.
  • Calm in errors: say what happened and what to do next.
  • Friendly, never cutesy, inside the product. Playful only in marketing.

PEECOOL BRAND GUIDELINES · v1.005
02Product name

Peecool

Pronunciation & meaning

PEE · cool

P for pickleball, cool for composed. Two short syllables, easy to say across a noisy facility and easy to spell from memory.

Why it was chosen

  • Proposed by the project owner after Ralvo turned out to be an existing app.
  • Cleared more checks than any other candidate: zero App Store matches; .com and .net confirmed unregistered; .app, .io and .ph looked unregistered (registrar check still needed).
  • Not tied to one feature, so it can grow past scoring.

Known concern: read this

  • “Pee” is a common word. Expect jokes, and be ready for some buyers to find it too casual for a business product.
  • Mitigation: the identity is serious (green, bold tabular numerals, checkmark symbol), the tagline leans on “cool”, and the co-branding rule puts the facility’s name first.
  • Test it with two or three real facility owners before committing to print, signage or a domain purchase.
  • Have a native Filipino speaker check for unintended meanings.

PEECOOL BRAND GUIDELINES · v1.010
03Logo

Backgrounds and misuse

✓ Primary on cream, white, mint
✓ Light on court green 700–950
✓ Dark on lime, sage
✓ On photos: light version on a dark overlay

Never

✕ Stretch or squash
✕ Rotate or tilt
✕ Off-palette colour
✕ Primary on lime
✕ Shadows or effects
✕ Low-contrast background

Also never: rearrange the symbol and wordmark, outline the letters, retype the name in another font, or add a tagline inside the clear space.

06Accessibility and consistency

Rules that keep it honest

Accessibility

  • Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1; scores ≥ 7:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA, as the spec requires)
  • Status never by colour alone: icon + label
  • Touch targets ≥ 44 px; console ≥ 64 px
  • Focus ring: 2 px, 2 px offset, brand colour per theme (kept from the app)
  • Reduced-motion honoured; the ticker and pulse stop
  • Body text ≥ 14 px; 12 px only for overlines
  • No information in hover-only states (tablets)

Consistency

  • Spell it Peecool (capital P only) in running text; the all-caps wordmark appears only inside the logo
  • Never abbreviate to “PC”: it collides with “Master PC”
  • All clock times 12-hour (5:05 PM), set explicitly, never by device locale
  • One status vocabulary across courts, matches, Open Play
  • Tokens only: no raw hex in components
  • Facility name first, “Powered by Peecool” second

07Open items

Unresolved before this is final

Naming and legal

  • Formal trademark search (USPTO, EUIPO, IPOPHL) by counsel: not done. Nothing here is legal clearance.
  • Checked only: US App Store, .com RDAP, web search. Not checked: Google Play, Microsoft Store, social handles, other countries’ stores.
  • On 2026-11-01 peecool.com and .net were unregistered (Verisign); .app, .io and .ph looked free but need a registrar check. Register promptly if approved.
  • The “pee” reading: test with real facility owners; ask a native Filipino speaker to check.

Design and product

  • Scoreboard legibility at 5 m on a real 10-inch tablet: simulated only
  • The client facility’s name and logo are not in the repo (the proposal says “[CLIENT / BUSINESS NAME]”): co-branding assumes “facility name first”
  • Print colour (Pantone, CMYK) not yet specified
  • The preview and slides show illustrative screens: no application screen was modified
